Finishes

Three finishes. One right answer for your job.

Stamped

A pattern pressed into fresh concrete for the look of stone.

Best for pool decks, patios and walkways.

Broom finish

Brushed while the concrete is still wet, so the surface has texture underfoot.

Best for driveways, sidewalks and steps.

Smooth

A flat, troweled finish for indoor floors.

Best for basements and garages.

Not sure which one? We go over all three at the visit.

Questions

Straight answers.

What does a concrete project cost?

It depends on size, access, the base and the finish, so we don't publish a number. After the visit you get a written estimate that lists every step and material.

Do you work in my town?

If it's within about 40 miles of Webster, most likely yes. That covers Worcester, Framingham and the towns in between. We don't work in Connecticut. Put your ZIP in the form and you'll see right away.

Do you take small jobs?

Close to Webster, yes. Farther out, we take larger pours. And we don't pour one or two steps on their own: the concrete truck costs the same either way, so a tiny job ends up priced too high for you.

How do payments work?

We take cash, check, and credit or debit cards. The payment terms are written into your contract.

Is there a warranty?

Yes. On request, we write a warranty into your contract. Honest note: over the years, any concrete can show hairline cracks. We cut control joints to guide where they happen, and it's rare that we need to go back to a job.

Do you work for builders and GCs?

Yes. We pour slabs, monolithic slabs and foundation additions for general contractors. See the Contractors page or call us.
