The estimate

What a retaining wall estimate covers

  1. Forms set to the height and length agreed
  2. Rebar, as your estimate lists
  3. The pour and the finish on the exposed face
  4. A patio or walkway on the new level ground, if you add one
Patio · Stamped

The wall is how you get the flat yard.

A slope you can't use becomes a level space for a patio, a play area or a garden. Pour the wall and the patio with the same crew and they're planned together from day one.

FAQ

Questions

Can you pour a patio on the level ground behind the wall?

Yes. Patios are one of our main jobs, and planning both together keeps the levels right.

What do you look at during the visit?

The slope, how long and how tall the wall needs to be, what it holds back, and how the concrete truck can get close.

How do payments work?

Cash, check, credit or debit card.

Is there a warranty?

On request, we write a warranty into your contract.

Turn the slope into yard.

Tell us about the slope and what you want on top. Retaining wall is already marked.
